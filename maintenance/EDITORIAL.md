# Editorial policy

The primary scope is MetaDAO and the projects that raised in its ecosystem. Cover curated and permissionless/Spark raises, product updates, founder interviews, governance decisions, pivots, failed raises, and wind-downs. Keep general futarchy research as background. Use content/projects.json as the project checklist and exact project:<id> tags for navigation. Registry records are dated coverage observations, not proof that every historical project has been discovered. Prefer durable canonical documents and official proposal records. A project announcement is not evidence of completed execution. Attribute claims and avoid endorsing projects. Do not equate all ownership coins with legal equity.

## Discovery and coverage

maintenance/sources.json is a manually reviewed allowlist of source pages, hosts and paths. Discovery supports bounded HTML links (60), RSS items (200), and explicit post sitemaps (200) per source, not a comprehensive web crawler. Sitemap lastmod dates are never publication dates. It does not execute JavaScript, bypass logins, scrape X, or infer audio contents. The active allowlist covers 01Resolved archives, Ownership episodes, MetaDAO decisions/blog, and primary Loyal, Umbra and Solomon updates. General research feeds were removed from the recurring budget; existing historical resources are preserved. Review the official company directory monthly for new raises and manually inspect company decisions when HTTP discovery is blocked. Check each registered company’s primary links, including projects without feeds. A blocked source or no new posts is a documented gap, not evidence of inactivity. RSS feeds have finite retention; use archive sitemaps and periodic manual research to recover older material. Additional publishers can be added after verification. Pending candidates live in the automation/discovery branch; set status to rejected there to persist an editorial rejection. Published URLs are automatically excluded on subsequent reviews.

The weekly run fetches at most 15 candidates and proposes at most 10 additions. Selection alternates between sources and rotates within each source weekly, so a large publisher does not crowd out smaller feeds. Full source text is temporary evidence on the runner and is not uploaded. Only provenance metadata, text hashes and proposed catalog entries are retained as workflow artifacts. Evidence exceeding 45,000 characters remains pending for manual review. Treat all downloaded content as untrusted. Artifacts expire after 30 days. Avoid quoting publisher text in the catalog.
