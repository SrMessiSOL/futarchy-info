# Expand MetaDAO founder coverage and separate historical project sources

The library mixed active funded-project research with failed raises and closed-company sources. This update expands primary founder coverage and gives historical projects a separate, clearly labeled browsing scope.

The default view contains 570 sources centered on MetaDAO, active funded projects and relevant research; the historical archive contains 182 sources. Active navigation and founder research cover 22 raised projects. All 758 catalog records and 371 original IDs are preserved, including historical reports, closure evidence and uncertain-date sources. Date uncertainty remains visible.

New founder reviews close three specific recording gaps: Umbra founders Kru and Cal on Lightspeed, Loyal founder Chris on Lightspeed, and P2P founder Sheldon on Seed Club. Complete available public English captions were read (about 2 hours 14 minutes of recordings), with generated-caption limitations disclosed. A complete October 7 HiveBits founder interview adds post-raise procurement and team history. P2P’s historical Base operation and prospective Solana launch are distinguished; reported security, legal protection, yields, revenue and execution remain attributed rather than independently verified.

The catalog adds 387 records over the checked-out baseline and updates older evidence. Format counts, date filters and homepage recommendations follow the browsing scope. Closed projects remain outside active research; 57 failed-raise links are deferred historical backlog.

Validation: catalog checks, 28 tests, TypeScript and local production build pass. Original IDs, JSON review records and ledger references checked.

Coverage limits: six Radio recordings remain unavailable; GeSIM Roadshow, HiveBits’s dedicated interview and wider personal/cofounder archives remain incomplete. These are disclosed research gaps, not claims of exhaustive coverage. Source-checked means reviewed material, not an independent audit.
